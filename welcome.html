Reading the colors

Placid
Flat water, barely a breeze.
Calm
Light wind, a small ripple.
Chop
Workable but bumpy, often wind against the tide.
Rough
Stay in. Conditions are unpleasant or dangerous. Includes Small Craft Advisories.

Where the numbers come from

Tides and currents

Tide data comes from National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration (NOAA) predictions. They follow the moon, so they hold months ahead.

Wind and weather

Forecast models. Sharpest for the next day or two, looser after that.

Waves

Calculated from the wind and how much open water it crosses, plus any National Weather Service advisories.
